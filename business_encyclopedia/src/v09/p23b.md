## Guide to business departments

| Department | Core mission | Key outputs | Typical KPIs | Main interfaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finance / accounting | Record, report, plan and control money | Financial statements, budgets, forecasts, management accounts | Close speed, forecast accuracy, DSO | All departments, auditors, banks |
| Treasury | Liquidity, funding, FX and interest-rate risk | Cash forecasts, hedges, bank facilities | Liquidity headroom, cost of funds | Finance, banks, sales, procurement |
| Tax | Compliance and efficient tax structure | Returns, transfer pricing files | Effective tax rate, no penalties | Finance, legal |

| Customer service / success | Retain and support customers | Resolved tickets, renewals | NPS, churn, resolution time | Sales, operations |
| Procurement | Buy the right inputs on the right terms | Contracts, POs, supplier panel | Savings, OTIF, supplier risk | Operations, finance, legal |
| Operations / production | Produce goods or deliver services | Output, quality | Yield, utilisation, cost per unit | Procurement, logistics, sales |
| Supply chain / logistics | Move and store goods | Shipments, inventory | OTIF, inventory days, freight cost | Procurement, sales, customs brokers |
| HR / people | Hire, develop, pay and retain people | Hiring, payroll, policies | Turnover, time-to-hire, engagement | All |
| Legal | Protect the company contractually and legally | Contracts, opinions, dispute handling | Turnaround, disputes avoided | Sales, procurement, finance |

“International standard” rarely means “automatically binding law”. ICC rules bind because the parties incorporate them; accounting and banking standards bind because national laws or regulators adopt them, sometimes with modifications. Always check the jurisdiction and the contract.
:::

## Master transaction checklist

Use this checklist before committing to any significant deal: sale, purchase, financing, investment or partnership.

:::framework 1. Counterparty
- Legal identity verified (registry extract, UBO, authorised signatories).
- KYC / sanctions / PEP screening completed and documented.
- Financial strength assessed (accounts, credit report, references).
- Track record and reputation checked; red flags investigated.
:::

:::framework 2. Commercial terms
- Product/service specification and quality standard precise.
- Quantity, tolerance, delivery schedule and place defined.
- Price, currency, adjustment mechanism and Incoterms® (with version and place) agreed.
- Payment terms and payment security agreed (L/C, guarantee, insurance, advance).
:::

:::framework 3. Economics
- Full landed cost / total cost of ownership calculated.
- Margin after financing, FX, insurance and contingencies positive.
- Sensitivity to price, FX, freight and delay tested; break-even known.
- Cash-flow timing mapped; working capital and funding available.
:::

:::framework 4. Risk
- Credit, FX, commodity, interest-rate, country and logistics risks identified.
- Hedging, insurance, collateral and guarantees arranged as needed.
- Worst-case loss quantified and within appetite.
:::

:::framework 5. Legal and compliance
- Contract reviewed: liability caps, LDs, force majeure, termination, governing law, dispute resolution.
- Export controls, licences, sanctions, customs classification and origin confirmed.
- Tax effects (VAT, withholding, transfer pricing, permanent establishment) checked with advisers.
- Required internal approvals obtained per delegation of authority.
:::

:::framework 6. Execution and control
- Responsibilities (RACI), timeline and documents list prepared.
- Documents checked against contract/L/C before presentation.
- Monitoring of shipment, payments, covenants and KPIs in place.
- Post-deal review scheduled; lessons recorded.
:::

## Personal knowledge assessment framework

| Level | Description | Evidence example |

| 1 — Recognise | Recognise terms when reading | Can match terms to definitions |

| 4 — Judge | Can choose between options and spot problems | Identify discrepancies; recommend a structure |
| 5 — Advise | Can lead a transaction or decision and defend it | Present a credit or investment recommendation |
